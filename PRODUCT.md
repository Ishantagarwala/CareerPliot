# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and early-career job seekers (India-first, English UI). They arrive uncertain about career direction and leave with a plan. Sessions are long: roadmap planning, AI tutoring, resume building, job hunting — often on laptops in dorms, sometimes on phones between classes. They are ambitious but overwhelmed; the product's job is to convert anxiety into a tracked, actionable path.

## Product Purpose

CareerPilot is an AI career-guidance platform: career assessment → personalized roadmaps → learning (AI tutor, study hub with document chat, courses) → resume builder + scoring → live job board → application tracker → progress dashboard. Success looks like a student going from "I don't know what to do" to "I'm 68% ready for this specific role, here's my next milestone."

## Brand Personality

**Bold · Warm · Driven.** A confident coach in your corner — energetic and encouraging, never corporate-stiff, never childish. The interface should feel like momentum: warm surfaces, decisive accents, progress you can see.

## Anti-references

- **Neo-brutalism** — hard black borders, offset block shadows, sticker aesthetics. This is the design being replaced.
- **Dark glassy AI cliché** — purple-gradient hero, glassmorphism-everywhere, glow-for-glow's-sake SaaS template.
- **Corporate dull** — enterprise HR-portal gray, lifeless tables, form-for-a-form's-sake.
- **Kiddie playground** — cartoons, kindergarten palettes, condescending copy.

## Design Principles

1. **Coach, not clown.** Bold and warm through typography, color commitment, and copy — not through mascots or decoration.
2. **Calm canvas, loud signal.** Surfaces stay quiet; one saturated accent carries every primary action. If everything pops, nothing does.
3. **Progress made visible.** Every screen answers "where am I, what's next" — streaks, readiness, milestones are first-class citizens.
4. **AI moments glow.** Gradients, glow, and motion are reserved for AI-generated content and AI states (streaming, thinking, generating). Static UI never competes with them.
5. **Long-session comfort.** Light-first, dark mode an equal citizen, real contrast, no visual fatigue over a two-hour study session.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥ 3:1. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation. Color never the sole carrier of meaning (status = icon + color). Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile.

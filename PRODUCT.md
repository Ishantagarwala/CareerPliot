# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and early-career professionals (e.g. BCA students) working toward a specific career goal — becoming a Full Stack Developer and similar. They use the hub in self-directed study sessions: uploading course PDFs, pasting notes, asking concept questions, building study plans, prepping for interviews. Sessions are focused, often long, and the chat IS the workspace.

## Product Purpose

Career Pilot's AI Study Hub is a personal study co-pilot. It helps users move toward a concrete career goal by explaining concepts, generating study plans, working with their uploaded materials, quizzing them, and supporting career prep. Success looks like: user returns daily, threads accumulate into a personal study archive, and the chat feels calm enough to think in.

## Brand Personality

Calm co-pilot, not a hype-man. Capable, warm, precise. The interface should feel like a good study desk: clean surface, everything within reach, nothing shouting. Personality in 3 words: calm, capable, personal.

## Anti-references

- Emoji-salesman AI greetings (walls of emoji bullets, "So, what would you like to tackle today? 🚀😊") — the model must not perform enthusiasm.
- Generic SaaS-chrome chat (gradient blobs, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids as filler).
- Neon-noise: the lime accent is a highlight, not a coat of paint — never fill large surfaces with it.
- Dense IDE/busy-ness: no grey-on-grey chrome, no badge spam, no "power user" clutter by default.

## Design Principles

1. **Quiet editorial.** Hierarchy comes from typography, spacing, and restraint — not boxes and borders. The reference direction (clean centered greeting, plain flowing message list) is the target feel.
2. **The message is the content.** AI replies render as flowing readable prose/markdown with generous line-height and controlled measure (~65-75ch); chrome recedes.
3. **Lime is an action, not a theme.** Accent reserved for primary actions and focus states (send, new chat, active selection). Surfaces stay neutral.
4. **Empty states carry the brand.** The first thing a user sees (greeting, input, suggestions) sets the tone — deterministic, personal, calm; never AI-generated confetti.
5. **Calm over clever.** Micro-motion is subtle (fade, translate), never bouncy; thinking/streaming indicators are quiet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum in both light and dark hub themes (lime on light surfaces never used for text; body text ≥4.5:1).
- Reduced-motion support for all reveal animations and streaming indicators.
- Full keyboard access: focus states on all interactive chrome; Enter-to-send, Esc-to-close conventions preserved.
